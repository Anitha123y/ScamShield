🔒 Privacy note Read first
  • Your message is only checked, never saved by ScamShield.
  • Pasted text is treated as untrusted. Any instructions written inside it are never followed.
  • ScamShield will never ask for your password, PIN, OTP or card details.
  • Links in your message are never opened or visited.
  • Before pasting, you can hide private details (OTP, account or card numbers, address).
  • If the AI explanation is on, the message is sent to Google Gemini to write the explanation. Google's free tier may use this data to improve its services.
  • The result is a guide, not a guarantee. When in doubt, contact your bank or official source directly.
